Rush Rafting Company

We believe that whitewater rafting should be accessible to everyone from first-time paddlers to experienced thrill-seekers. Our mission is to create unforgettable adventures that challenge the mind, invigorate the spirit, and build lasting memories. Every trip is an opportunity to connect with nature and discover your inner strength.

History

Rush Rafting Company was founded in 2026 with a passion for adventure and a commitment to safety. What began as a small operation with just two guides has grown into a trusted leader in whitewater rafting. Over the years, we've guided thousands of adventurers through thrilling rapids while maintaining our core values of safety, respect for nature, and exceptional customer service.

Beyond providing thrilling adventures, we're committed to protecting the waterways and natural environments we work in. We follow Leave No Trace principles on every trip and actively participate in river conservation efforts. We also believe in supporting our local community by employing local guides and contributing to youth adventure programs.
