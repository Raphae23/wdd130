Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring?

Bring a change of clothes, towel, sunscreen, and water shoes. We provide all safety equipment and protective gear.

Are refunds available?

Yes, we offer full refunds for cancellations made 48 hours before your trip. For cancellations within 48 hours, a 50% refund applies.

Do you offer group discounts?

Absolutely! Groups of 10 or more receive a 15% discount on most trips. Contact us for custom group packages.
